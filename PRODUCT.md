# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

Personal portfolio of Aditya Sahni: full-stack developer, CS undergrad at McMaster
University, builder of systems that touch live markets and real money. Flagship work:
MapleRewards (Canadian credit-card rewards optimizer, Go + Next.js), an autonomous
13-agent Polymarket trading bot with net-positive realized P&L, Life-OS (gamified
personal operating system), and from-scratch paper reimplementations (Raft, Shazam
fingerprinting, sparse autoencoders).

## Register

brand

Design IS the product here. The portfolio is the proof of frontend taste; a hiring
manager or client should feel the craft before reading a word.

## Users

- Recruiters and engineering managers screening candidates (30-second skim, then deep-dive)
- Freelance clients evaluating whether he can build their product
- Fellow builders arriving from GitHub or LinkedIn

## Brand and tone

Confident, edited, print-literate. The site reads like a designed monograph of
the work: warm paper, ink-black grotesque type at poster scale, hairline rules,
one vermilion accent. Real numbers and real screenshots do the persuading.
Explicitly NOT: dark dashboards, terminal cosplay, green-on-black, or anything
that could be mistaken for a trading platform.

## Anti-references

- The space-template portfolio this replaced (purple black-hole video, starfield,
  typewriter intro): everything it did, do the opposite.
- Generic SaaS landing pages (centered hero, three feature cards, purple gradients).
- Dribbble-style over-illustrated portfolios with mascots and blobs.
- Anything that looks like it shipped from a YouTube tutorial.

## Strategic principles

1. Show the work, not claims about the work: real screenshots, real metrics, real repos.
2. Terminal/trading-console aesthetic as identity: signal-green on near-black, mono
   accents, system-status furniture.
3. Motion with purpose: scroll should feel like moving through a system, not
   decoration bolted on.
4. Every page renders from one typed data file; content changes never touch layout code.
